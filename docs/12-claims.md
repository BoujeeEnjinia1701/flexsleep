# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod claims register

The pod supports IPC practice by giving each bed a physical boundary and visual privacy; it makes no claim that it prevents infection or transmission. Claims rule: `infection` (STANDARDS 25.5b).

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.

## May claim (on paper evidence, FXS-STM-001)

- A physical boundary between beds against direct contact and against large droplets thrown sideways (S1).
- Visual privacy for a patient lying down from most of the aisle; not for a patient sitting up; visual only, not acoustic (S5, S6, S51).
- Open top and open foot end; staff observe from the foot end (S7).
- Fits field cots 60 to 100 cm wide (S8).
- Opens at the head without tools by releasing two clips (S17, estimate to be timed).
- Built from flame-retardant panels bought with NFPA 701 TM1 and ASTM E84 reports (as benchmarks, S20).
- Folds flat: four panels stack to about 40 mm (S25).

## May claim only after testing

- Any reduction in airborne particle exchange with the next bed (decision gate V1; S2).
- Set-up time by one person (V2; S24) and head release time (V2; S17).
- Thermal comfort in warm wards (V3; S4, S44).
- Survival of 50 deployments and of disinfectant wipes (FXP-PLT-001 T2 to T4).
- Fire behaviour beside a bed (D7 test).

## Never claim

- No claim that it prevents infection, transmission or contamination, or is infection-proof.
- No claim that it is an isolation room or ward, airborne isolation, or negative pressure.
- No claim that it protects against any disease, or kills or eliminates pathogens.
- No claim that it is sterile, medical-grade, or FDA or CE approved.
- No claim that it is a barrier against people or fluids, a bed rail, a grab rail or a support.
- No claim that it is fire-safe or fireproof.

## On-product wording (proposed, awaiting Amish)

- Inside each side wall: prohibition sign and "Do not lean or pull. Ask staff to help you stand."
- Head joint: "Release here to open the head."
- Bag label: "Privacy and boundary pod. Not a barrier or a support. Keep flames, heaters, lamps, chargers and oxygen equipment away. Indoor or in-tent use. Not for patient care until reviewed by IPC and safety staff."
