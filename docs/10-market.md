# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod market and viability

A pod costs about USD 214 in parts at prototype quantities, USD 4.28 a deployment over 50; the value-engineering target is USD 120 and the stretch USD 75. The nearest commercial answers, FR vinyl privacy screens, cost about USD 211 to 236 each and screen one side of one bed. The pod's niche is surge wards and shelters that want privacy and a contact boundary at every bed, can accept about 38 % fewer beds per tent, and will set the fire rules it needs. Prices are cited with the date checked; none is a quote.

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.

## Open route

Open hardware under CERN-OHL-P-2.0: anyone may make and sell the pod. Builders: sign shops (cutting, routing and printing FR twinwall), makerspaces for clips and feet, logistics bases that stock Flex platform parts shared with FlexClean and FlexTriage. Cost falls with sheet bought by the pallet, shared freight and moulded clips and feet (FXP-PLT-001 D4).
