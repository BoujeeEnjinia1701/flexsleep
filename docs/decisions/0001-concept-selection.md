# 0001: Concept selection

Status: **Proposed, awaiting Amish** (FXS-DEC-001 D1).

## Context

Three working principles were modelled at D3 and scored against FXS-URS-001 (FXS-OPT-001): FS1 origami panel pod (team concept, iteration 2), FS2 fabric privacy screen on a pole frame, FS3 cot-mounted head-end screen.

## Options and scores

FS3 171, FS1 149, FS2 141 of 235. FS3 is 15 % above FS1, so the two are not within 10 %; FS1 and FS2 are within 10 % of each other.

## Recommendation

Develop FS3 at the next stage, grafting FS1's FR twinwall panels and printed faces; FS1 stays the carried design in the drawings, BOM and build plan until Amish decides, because the team's concept is not dropped or redesigned without his go (STANDARDS 21).

## Why FS3 leads, with numbers

- The cot carries every load: FS1's free ends tip at about 11 N and a patient pulling to stand needs 137 to 281 N (FXS-STM-001 S10, S11); FS2's side frames tip at 3.5 to 12.4 N (X2).
- No floor beyond the cot: FS1 needs 2.09 x 2.86 m, about 6.2 pods per 45 m2 against 10 beds (S31).
- Less fire load: about 0.6 kg of FR fabric against 4 kg of PP sheet (X9, S20).

## What FS3 must still show

Lift-off release in seconds (X8), clamp fit to each cot type (X6), privacy beyond the head end (X7), fire report of its fabric, and freedom to operate against US 11,369,208 B2 and US 12,213,925 B2 (X10, FXS-FTO-001).

## Consequences

If Amish chooses FS1, the open decisions D2, D5 and D7 decide whether it can be used round sleeping patients at all. If he chooses FS3, a new model, BOM and build plan follow at P3 in a later session.
