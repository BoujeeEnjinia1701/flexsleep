# 0002: Design for construction of FS1

Status: made under STANDARDS section 18; reversible by Amish.

## Changes from the team's concept

| Concept (iteration 2) | Constructable design | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three panels (head, left, right) | Four P12 duos in two mirror halves; the head is two leaves meeting on an angle joint | Platform modules are 600 mm leaves and 1,200 mm duos; a split head is what opens for resuscitation |
| Flat side panels | Zigzag side walls with 30 deg folds and angle joints | Platform rule S1: every panel edge over 600 mm supported |
| "Snap connectors" | Platform angle clips on a round core | The platform square core blocks the 150 deg slot by 0.93 mm (F1) |
| Feet not drawn | Four platform foot blocks with pinned outriggers, EPDM pads and 5 kg bags; none at the head centre | Platform rule S3 and ballast rule; the outrigger needed a fixing (F3); the head must swing (F5) |
| Free wall ends | Aluminium edge channels bolted through the outer clip holes | A free-edged leaf buckles hot (F7) |
| Joint gaps not drawn | Clear PP cover strips on both faces | 11.6 mm slits at each angle joint (F8) |
| Semi-transparent upper panels | Not carried; 1.2 m walls seen over | Height and claims to Amish (D3) |
| Perforated lower band | Carried, forty 40 mm holes at 350 mm | Team feature; drop proposed (D4) |

## How each part is made

Sheet blanks are cut, routed, hot-punched and heat-sealed by a sign shop; clips and foot blocks are printed in PETG; outriggers are folded from offcut; channels are cut from aluminium stock and drilled; strips are cut from clear PP film. Every made part has a making sketch (FXS-DWG-101 to 105) and the printed parts an STL in `cad/stl/`.
