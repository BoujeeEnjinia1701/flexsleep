# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod feasibility assessment

FS1 is feasible to make and assemble with sign-shop and makerspace skills; it is not yet feasible to use round sleeping patients, because two first-order safety cases (a patient's pull and a bed fire) fail on paper and go to Amish (FXS-DEC-001 D2, D7).

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.

| Aspect | Assessment | Rating | Risks and notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Plan, joints and fit worked out on the platform; constructable at D3 (cad/fidelity.json) | High | Platform requests 1 to 4 (REVIEW) |
| Materials | FR twinwall at 700 g/m2 with reports exists (FXP-PLT-001); PETG, A4 stainless, aluminium channel, clear PP strip are stock items | Medium | FR report on the bought lot (C1); halogen-free FR (FXP-PLT-001 D11) |
| Manufacturing and assembly | Two sheets, six clips, four blocks; 4.2 to 8.3 min set-up estimated | High | Timed trial V2 |
| Power | None | n/a | n/a |
| Controls | None | n/a | n/a |
| Supply | Sign shops and makerspaces worldwide; freight of 48 x 96 in sheets by motor freight | Medium | Cash outlay about USD 600 for a first prototype |
| Safety in use | Wall gives way under a pull (S11); fire case beside bedding (S20); tips at about 11 N (S10) | Low | Decisions D2, D5, D7 |
| Clinical fit | Head opens in 8 to 20 s (estimate); routine care from the foot end; about 38 % fewer beds | Medium | D6, D8; drills with a field team |
| Evidence for the team's airborne hypothesis | Weak and mixed in the literature (S2) | Low | Decision-gate test V1 |
