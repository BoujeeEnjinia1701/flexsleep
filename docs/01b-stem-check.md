# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod STEM check

Verdict: the origami pod (option FS1) works as **a visual screen for a lying patient and a physical boundary against direct contact and sideways large droplets**, and nothing in it breaks known science, but it is not a barrier, not a support and not a proven airborne measure, and it is the hardest fire case of the platform. **Particles:** the walls stop 84.9 % of horizontal directions from the mouth of a patient lying on the cot (where a cough goes when the patient is on their side), but a supine cough goes up through the open top, and aerosols follow the room air; the cited studies give no basis for promising an airborne effect, so it is claimed only after the decision-gate test (S1, S2). **Heat:** a sleeping to seated patient (72 to 108 W) drives a plume of about 52 to 59 m3/h out of the open top, 4.1 to 5.4 K above the ward, so heat is not trapped; the real thermal cost is lost air movement from fans and draughts, not yet quantified (S4, S44). **Sightlines:** at 1.21 m the walls hide a lying patient from most of the aisle but not from a tall person within 0.64 m, and a seated patient's head shows; staff observe from the open foot end (S5 to S7). **Cots:** the pod fits cots 600 to 1,000 mm wide with 285 to 85 mm a side and 1.9 to 2.0 m long with the feet out (S8, S9). **Stability:** the free end of a side wall tips at about 11 N and a patient needs 137 to 281 N to pull up to stand, so a wall gives way under a patient's pull; this fails and goes to Amish with a prohibition sign fitted now (S10, S11). **Fire:** the pod adds about 201 MJ beside a 144 MJ foam mattress, 1.4 times the bed's own load, and its walls stand inside the mattress flame; the fire case goes to Amish with pods kept apart and no ignition sources (S20). **Resuscitation:** two clips release the head, whose leaves swing open on their folds in about 8 to 20 s with 0.6 m kept clear behind the head (S17). Of 51 rows for FS1, 15 are within known science, 28 plausible and waiting for TRL 4 evidence, and 8 fail with a fix that goes to Amish (S3, S6, S7, S10, S11, S18, S20, S40); none fails outright. `stem_check: open`. Method: STANDARDS sections 20 and 25.6a (structures); calculations in `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py` and `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (FXS-CAL-001). Platform claims (sheet stiffness, clips, folds, disinfection, FR grade) are checked once in FXP-PLT-001 (rows P1 to P40) and cited here. The pod supports IPC practice; it makes no claim on transmission (FXS-CLM-001).

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.

## Fixes made under the standing instruction (2026-10-23)

Each keeps the working principle (pre-scored FR polypropylene panels folding round a cot, tool-free clip-lock joints, open top, printed faces) and is recorded in FXS-DEC-001 and FXS-DDR-003.

- **F1 Angle clip on a round core.** The platform helper's square core stands 0.93 mm proud of the stop line in the 150 deg slot, so the panel's holes missed the pegs (S14). The same arms sit on a 12 mm round core.
- **F2 Prohibition sign** with the words "Do not lean or pull. Ask staff to help you stand." on both side walls at 700 to 900 mm, in ISO 3864 prohibition form (red circle and bar, not yellow) (S11, S34).
- **F3 Pinned outriggers.** The platform outrigger butted against its block and carried no moment; a 100 mm spigot on the block and a 6 mm stainless pin now join them (S15).
- **F4 EPDM pads** bonded under every foot block (S12).
- **F5 Split head that swings open.** No foot at the head centre; after the two centre clips are released both head leaves swing 90 deg outward on their corner folds, so nothing is lifted or slid. First drawn as a half that slid away, which would have tipped over first (S17).
- **F6 Pod spacing and ignition rules.** Pods never touch, so no continuous wall joins beds; no heat sources, candles, coils, lamps, chargers, oxygen cylinders or concentrators in or on a pod; oxygen tubing over the nearest wall top (S20 to S22).
- **F7 Edge stiffener channels** bolted over each free edge, so the terminal leaf is held on both edges (S16: a free edge buckles at 50 C).
- **F8 Joint cover strips** on both faces of every angle joint, over the 11.6 mm slit (S1, S36, S39).
- **F9 Head clearance** of 0.6 m behind the head wall, so the head leaves can swing and the clips can be reached (S17).
- Pins and bolts in A4 (316) stainless for hypochlorite wipes (S27).

## Independent review

A separate agent, run as its own process on 2026-11-12 (`claude -p`, Opus) with only `stem_results.csv`, `stem_results_options.csv`, `stem_check.py`, `sizing.py`, `sizing_results.json`, `cad/src/model.py`, `bom/bom.csv` and the cited sources (not this document and not docs/platform/), tried to refute every verdict. Of 49 rows it found 28 holding, 17 weak and 4 refuted (S16, S17, S31, S34), and listed 22 missed claims. It fetched Rooney, Li, MSF and Bolli; it could not open the ACP Hospitalist and Staples pages (HTTP 403) or the NCFS mattress table (TLS); those figures were read by the author on 2026-11-12 and stand as unverified by the reviewer, not refuted. I checked each finding with numbers before acting on it. What changed:

- **S16 (refuted, confirmed):** `platform_check.py` computes the flat-strip factor as (h_cr / H)^3 with no load factor (line 143), so a free-edged P12 leaf has a raw factor of 1.73 cold and 0.87 hot: it would buckle in a hot tent. Fixed by the edge channels (F7); with both edges held the platform's leaf margin is 4.7 cold and 2.4 hot at 1.2 m, load factor 2 included. Platform request 3.
- **S17 (refuted, confirmed):** a released half with two 5 kg bags tips at about 22 N before it slides at 50 to 75 N, so "slide one half away" failed. Fixed by F5 and F9 (swing open, no centre foot, 0.6 m behind the head): about 8 to 20 s estimated.
- **S31 (refuted, confirmed):** the footprint left out the cot end; now 2.09 x 2.86 m with the cot and head clearance, about 6.2 pods in a 45 m2 tent against 10 beds, about 38 % fewer (the reviewer's 30 to 32 % used a smaller head allowance).
- **S34 (refuted, confirmed):** "do not lean or pull" is a prohibition (ISO 3864-1 red circle and bar), not a yellow warning; F2 redrawn.
- **Weak rows restated with numbers:** S1 (supine cough goes up; head wall 0.45 m; joint slits), S2 (Rooney quote corrected; King and Gilkeson added), S4 (point-source estimate; fever; lost air movement moved to S44), S5 (tall person within 0.64 m sees the face; 0.5 m between pods), S6 and S7 (now "Fails, fix proposed": the team claims fail as stated and restating them is Amish's), S12 (a half tips before it slides), S13 (top clip takes 82 % by lever arm: up to 231 N against 176 N), S15 (outrigger bending 11.3 MPa against 24 MPa), S21 (neighbour distance 1.76 m; the burning pod walls are the threat), S22 (oxygen enrichment computed: up to 23.6 % at 15 L/min with half the exchange), S24 (bags filled in advance; occupied cot needs two), S27 (A4 stainless), S28 (slits, clip crevices), S36 and S37, X2 (FS2 with ballast 12.4 N), X3 (UL 94 is not a curtain test), X4 (USD 421.58).
