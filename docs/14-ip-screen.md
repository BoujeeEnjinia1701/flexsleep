# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod patent screen

Not legal advice. A desk screen on Google Patents on 2026-11-12, reading the abstract, status and first claim of each record. FS1 (a freestanding, open-top panel pod round a cot) is distant from the records found; FS3 (a cot-mounted screen) sits close to two active patents and needs an attorney's opinion before it is developed (FXS-DEC-001 C8).

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.

| Patent | Title, owner | Status (Google, an assumption) | What it claims | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US 7,216,381 B1 | Portable privacy enclosure; I. Setzer | Expired (adjusted expiry 2025-03-01) | A bottom panel with folding side and end panels held upright by fasteners round a person, open top, opaque screens | Close in idea to FS1 but expired; FS1 has no bottom panel |
| US 9,326,902 B2 | Portable enclosure for a bed; Vivax Medical | Active, to 2034-02-28 | Frame with an adjustable-length upper support and upstanding end supports carrying a canopy by collars | Different from FS1 (no frame or canopy); near FS2 and FS3 frames; to assess |
| US 11,077,005 B1 | Patient isolation system and method; Duke University | Expired, fee related | Bed-mounted collapsible frame with drape, glove sleeves, filters and a fan for negative pressure | Not FS1; FS3 has no drape enclosure, filters or fan |
