# FlexSleep Origami Patient Pod regulatory and standards register

The pod makes no medical device claim and is designed as a room furnishing that supports IPC practice; any device classification is to assess with a regulatory consultant. The governing questions are fire performance of combustible panels beside bedding, and whether a deployment authority treats the panels as furnishings, curtains or interior finish. Standards shared with FlexClean and FlexTriage are researched once in FXP-PLT-001 and cited here.

Release limitation (STANDARDS 25.5b): no prototype is used in patient care until its stability, fire performance, cleanability and the procedure it embeds are reviewed by qualified IPC and safety staff.
